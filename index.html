Research

Two major research projects I have been working on.

DispSAC Poster in PIM (Passive Imaging and Monitoring in Wave Physics:From Seismology to Ultrasound (Asia-Pacific) Summer School)

Research Gap: SWI based on MCMC is very time intensive; Linearized inversion is very easily stuck into local minimum; SWI based on Supervised Learning ignores non-uniqueness nature of inverse problem

Solution: To addrease this problem, We propose DispSAC to fully explore parameter space by leveraging fewer forword simulations over MCMC

Related publication: [DOI: https://doi.org/10.31223/X5MJ75]

CCFRL for direct inference of shear wave velocity from ambient noise cross correlations

[Research Gap: Traditional Ambient Noise tomography extracts dispersion measurements before inverting for velocity structure, making the final models sensitive to dispersion picking and quality control.]

[Solution: We present CCFRL, a reinforcement-learning framework that estimates an effective one-dimensional shear-wave velocity model directly from a preprocessed interstation CCF, which skips the dispersion picking process directly]

Related publication: Still working and polishing
